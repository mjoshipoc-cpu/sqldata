The Problem "NCQA requires health plans to review patient records — called EMRs — across HEDIS measures. Each measure has complex rules that need clinical expertise to apply. These records come in all formats — digital, handwritten, unstructured. Reading them manually is slow and error prone. And it's not just about finding a procedure name. A Mammogram mentioned in a record doesn't mean it was performed. Clinical staff have to confirm it actually happened. On top of that — the same record gets reviewed multiple times for different measures. Today, this entire process is done manually — it's time consuming, costly, and inconsistent." ________________________________________ The Solution "We built an AI pipeline . Where NLP and LLM work together. Tool extracts the clinical data and reasons over it — understanding context, applying NCQA rules, and making the right abstraction decision. Not just finding keywords — understanding what happened. After that there is a Human-in-the-Loop to review to make sure the quality of extraction And finally — a Summarization Chatbot. Anyone can ask a question in plain English and get an instant answer from the document. No more manual searching." ________________________________________ Value to Business Accuracy goes up — from around 75% manually to 90-95% with AI. The chatbot saves 60-70% of document search time — what took 30 minutes now takes seconds. And for leadership — instead of waiting days for reports, get real-time dashboards. Close gaps faster.
