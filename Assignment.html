🔵 Chronic Care Measures

These measures track long-term management of conditions like hypertension, diabetes, cholesterol and older adults’ chronic needs.

Code Measure Name Meaning (Short)
CBP Controlling High Blood Pressure Percentage of hypertensive members whose blood pressure is under control.
BPD Diastolic BP Control Diastolic component of blood pressure under control in chronic population.
A1C Hemoglobin A1c Control Diabetes control measure based on A1c results over the last 3 months.
LDL Cholesterol Control LDL/cholesterol management, often linked to statin therapy adherence.
COA Care for Older Adults Includes medication review, functional status assessment and pain assessment for older adults.

🟢 Preventive Care Measures

Measures focused on screenings, counseling, and early detection to prevent complications.

Code Measure Name Meaning (Short)
WCC Weight Assessment & Counseling BMI/weight assessment with nutrition and physical activity counseling.
LSC Lipid Screening Preventive screening for cholesterol and lipid disorders.
PPC Prenatal & Postpartum Care Measures timeliness and completeness of prenatal and postpartum visits.
TRC Transitions of Care Follow-up and coordination after hospital discharge; often treated as preventive follow-up.

🟣 Care Coordination & Access

Measures that capture how well care is coordinated across settings and over time.

TRC – Transitions of Care
Ensures timely follow-up and medication reconciliation after discharge. High TRC performance is associated with lower readmission risk.

Care Coordination Score (example):
CareCoord_Score = TRC_Score

🟤 Behavioral Health

Measures related to mental health screening and management.

GSD – Depression Screening
Identifies members who may have depression or emotional distress. Early detection improves adherence and overall outcomes.

Behavioral Score (example):
Behavioral_Score = GSD_Score

📏 Gap Status & Gap Closure

A “gap” indicates whether a quality measure is met or unmet for a member.

  • Gap Value: Usually stored as Y, N, NA
  • Y = gap closed (measure met)
  • N = gap open (measure not met)
  • NA = member not eligible for the measure

Eligible Gaps: All records where Gap ∈ {Y, N}

Gap Closure % (member/provider level):

Gap Closure % = (# of Y) / (# of Y + # of N) × 100

🟠 Utilization & Cost KPIs

These metrics capture healthcare resource usage and cost efficiency at provider or population level.

Admit Rate per 1000
AdmitRate_1k = (Total Hospital Admits / Total Members) × 1000
Lower is better – fewer inpatient admissions.

ER Visit Rate per 1000
ER_1k = (Total ER Visits / Total Members) × 1000
Lower is better – fewer emergency room visits.

Readmission Rate
ReadmissionRate = Total Readmissions (30-day) / Total Hospital Admits
Lower is better – indicates good discharge planning and follow-up.

PMPM – Per Member Per Month
PMPM = Total Cost / Member Months
Core cost KPI. Lower PMPM = more efficient care for comparable quality.

⭐ Composite Scores & VBC

Composite scores combine multiple measures into a single index for each domain, then into an overall VBC score.

Chronic Score

Uses CBP, BPD, A1C, LDL, COA. Each measure is converted to a score (for example, 1 − Gap or % gap closed), then averaged: 

Chronic_Score = Average(CBP_Score, BPD_Score, A1C_Score, LDL_Score, COA_Score)

Preventive Score

Uses WCC, LSC, PPC, TRC: 

Preventive_Score = Average(WCC_Score, LSC_Score, PPC_Score, TRC_Score)

Care Coordination Score

CareCoord_Score = TRC_Score

Behavioral Score

Behavioral_Score = GSD_Score

Utilization Score

Built from domain-specific scores like AdmitScore, ERScore, ReadmissionScore, CostScore: 

Utilization_Score = Average(AdmitScore, ERScore, ReadmissionScore, CostScore)

VBC Composite Score (Example Weighting)

Example domain weights (can be tuned to contract requirements):

  • Chronic: 40%
  • Preventive: 25%
  • Care Coordination: 10%
  • Behavioral: 5%
  • Utilization & Cost: 20%
VBC_Score =
  0.40 * Chronic_Score +
  0.25 * Preventive_Score +
  0.10 * CareCoord_Score +
  0.05 * Behavioral_Score +
  0.20 * Utilization_Score

Final_VBC_Score (0–100) = VBC_Score * 100

🏅 Provider Tiering (Example)

Providers can be classified into tiers based on Final VBC Score:

  • Gold: Final VBC Score ≥ 80
  • Silver: 65–79
  • Bronze: < 65

These tiers can be used for incentives, recognition, network management, and targeted improvement programs.

🔥 Most Important Concepts

  • Chronic Score has the highest weight.
  • PMPM is the key cost KPI.
  • ER / 1000 and Admit / 1000 show avoidable utilization.
  • Gap Closure % drives quality performance.

🧠 How to Read Scores

  • All domain scores (Chronic, Preventive, etc.) are on 0–1 scale.
  • Final VBC Score is on 0–100 scale.
  • Higher = better quality and value.
  • Use tiering (Gold/Silver/Bronze) for quick comparisons.

💡 Interpretation Hints

  • Low Chronic + high ER/admit → poor chronic management.
  • High Preventive but low Chronic → early work done, but control still weak.
  • Low TRC & high readmission → poor care coordination.
  • High Behavioral gaps (GSD) → consider mental health outreach.

Note: This glossary is for analytics and VBC program interpretation. Exact clinical specifications are based on HEDIS/plan policies.
