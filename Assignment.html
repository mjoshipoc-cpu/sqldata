Objective The Utilization Score is a composite metric used to evaluate how efficiently healthcare services are utilized within a population. It combines key utilization indicators: • ER Visits • Hospital Admissions • 30-day Readmissions 👉 Purpose: • Measure cost efficiency • Identify care gaps • Support provider performance evaluation ________________________________________ 🔷 2. Components of Utilization Score ✅ 2.1 ER Visits per 1000 📌 Definition Number of emergency room visits per 1,000 members. 📌 Formula SUM(ER_Visits) / COUNT(*) * 1000 📌 Business Meaning • High ER visits → Possible lack of primary care / care gaps • Low ER visits → Better care coordination ________________________________________ ✅ 2.2 Admit Rate per 1000 📌 Definition Number of hospital admissions per 1,000 members. 📌 Formula SUM(Hospital_Admits) / COUNT(*) * 1000 📌 Business Meaning • High admissions → Higher cost, poor disease management • Low admissions → Efficient healthcare utilization ________________________________________ ✅ 2.3 Readmission Rate 📌 Definition Percentage of patients readmitted within 30 days of discharge. 📌 Formula SUM(Readmission_30_Day) / SUM(Hospital_Admits) 📌 Business Meaning • High readmission → Poor discharge planning / quality issues • Low readmission → Better care continuity ________________________________________ ✅ 2.4 Supporting Metrics (Volume) SUM(Hospital_Admits) AS Hospital_Admits SUM(Readmission_30_Day) AS Readmission_30 👉 Used for: • Reporting • Drill-down analysis ________________________________________ 🔷 3. Standardization (Why per 1000?) Metrics are calculated per 1000 members to: • Normalize population differences • Enable comparison across providers/regions • Improve interpretability 👉 Example: "100 admissions per 1000 members" is easier to interpret than raw ratios ________________________________________ 🔷 4. Scoring Logic (Normalization to 0–1 Scale) Each metric is converted into a standard score. ________________________________________ ✅ 4.1 Admit Rate Score 1 - ( case when Admit_Rate_per_1000 < 50 then 1 when Admit_Rate_per_1000 >= 50 and < 100 then 0.5 else 0 end ) 📊 Thresholds Admit Rate Score Interpretation < 50 0 Good 50–100 0.5 Moderate > 100 1 Poor ________________________________________ ✅ 4.2 ER Score 1 - ( case when ER_Visits_per_1000 < 50 then 1 when ER_Visits_per_1000 >= 50 and < 100 then 0.5 else 0 end ) 📊 Thresholds ER Visits Score Interpretation < 50 0 Good 50–100 0.5 Moderate > 100 1 Poor ________________________________________ ✅ 4.3 Readmission Score 1 - ( case when Readmission_Rate < 0.10 then 1 when Readmission_Rate >= 0.10 and < 0.20 then 0.5 else 0 end ) 📊 Thresholds Readmission Rate Score Interpretation < 10% 0 Good 10–20% 0.5 Moderate > 20% 1 Poor ✅ (Corrected scale for consistency) ________________________________________ 🔷 5. Why 1 - CASE is Used 📌 Purpose: To invert scoring direction Before After (Final Score) Good = 1 0 (Best) Medium = 0.5 0.5 Poor = 0 1 (Worst) 👉 Ensures: • Lower score = better performance • Higher score = worse performance ✅ Makes aggregation and interpretation consistent ________________________________________ 🔷 6. Final Utilization Score 📌 Formula ROUND( (Admit_Ratescore + ERscore + Readmissionscore) / 3, 2) AS UtilizationScore ________________________________________ ✅ Interpretation Score Range Meaning 0 – 0.3 Efficient utilization ✅ 0.3 – 0.7 Moderate ⚠️ 0.7 – 1 High utilization ❌ ________________________________________ 🔷 7. End-to-End Flow Raw Data (Claims) ↓ Calculate Metrics (ER, Admit, Readmission) ↓ Standardize (per 1000) ↓ Apply Thresholds (CASE) ↓ Invert Score (1 - CASE) ↓ Average All Scores ↓ Final Utilization Score ________________________________________ 🔷 8. Business Use Cases ✅ Used In: • Provider scorecards • HEDIS reporting • Population health dashboards • Risk stratification models ________________________________________ ✅ Helps Answer: • Which providers have high utilization? • Where are avoidable ER visits increasing? • Which population has poor care management? ________________________________________ 🔷 9. Key Insights ✅ Low utilization score → • Better care coordination • Lower cost • Improved outcomes ❌ High utilization score → • Overuse of services • Higher cost burden • Potential care gaps
