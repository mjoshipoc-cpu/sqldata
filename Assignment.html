🔵 Chronic Care Measures

These measures track long-term management of hypertension, diabetes, cholesterol and older adult chronic care.

Code Measure Name Meaning (Short)
CBP Chronic Controlling High Blood Pressure Percentage of hypertensive members whose blood pressure is under control.
BPD Chronic Diastolic BP Control Control of the diastolic component of blood pressure in chronic population.
A1C Chronic Hemoglobin A1c Control Diabetes control based on A1c results over the last ~3 months.
LDL Chronic Cholesterol Control LDL/cholesterol management, often tied to statin therapy adherence.
COA Chronic Care for Older Adults Medication review, functional status assessment and pain assessment for older adults.

🟢 Preventive Care Measures

Measures focused on screenings, counseling and early detection to prevent future complications.

Code Measure Name Meaning (Short)
WCC Preventive Weight Assessment & Counseling BMI/weight assessment with nutrition and physical activity counseling.
LSC Preventive Lipid Screening Preventive screening for cholesterol and lipid disorders.
PPC Preventive Prenatal & Postpartum Care Timeliness and completeness of prenatal and postpartum visits.
TRC PreventiveCoord Transitions of Care Follow-up and coordination after hospital discharge; reduces readmission risk.

🟣 Care Coordination & Access

Measures that capture how well care is coordinated across settings and over time.

TRC – Transitions of Care Care Coordination
Ensures timely follow-up and medication reconciliation after discharge. High TRC performance is associated with lower readmission and better continuity.

Care Coordination Score (example):
CareCoord_Score = TRC_Score

🟤 Behavioral Health

Measures related to mental health screening and management.

GSD – Depression Screening Behavioral
Screens members for depression or emotional distress. Early detection improves chronic control and adherence.

Behavioral Score (example):
Behavioral_Score = GSD_Score

📏 Gap Status & Gap Closure

A “gap” indicates whether a quality measure is met or unmet for a member.

  • Gap Value is usually Y, N or NA
  • Y = gap closed (measure met)
  • N = gap open (measure not met)
  • NA = member not eligible for that measure

Eligible Gaps = all records where Gap ∈ {Y, N}

Gap Closure % (member / provider level):

Gap Closure % = (# of Y) / (# of Y + # of N) × 100

🟠 Utilization & Cost KPIs

These KPIs capture resource use and cost efficiency at provider or population level.

Admit Rate per 1000 Utilization
AdmitRate_1k = (Total Hospital Admits / Total Members) × 1000
Lower is better (fewer inpatient admissions).

ER Visit Rate per 1000 Utilization
ER_1k = (Total ER Visits / Total Members) × 1000
Lower is better (fewer emergency room visits).

Readmission Rate Utilization
ReadmissionRate = Total 30-day Readmissions / Total Hospital Admits
Lower is better – indicates good discharge planning and follow-up.

PMPM – Per Member Per Month Cost
PMPM = Total Cost / Member Months
Core cost KPI. Lower PMPM = more efficient care at similar or better quality.

⭐ Composite Scores & VBC

Composite scores combine multiple measures into a single 0–1 score for each domain, then into an overall VBC score.

Chronic Score

Uses CBP, BPD, A1C, LDL, COA:

Chronic_Score = Average(CBP_Score, BPD_Score, A1C_Score, LDL_Score, COA_Score)

Preventive Score

Uses WCC, LSC, PPC, TRC:

Preventive_Score = Average(WCC_Score, LSC_Score, PPC_Score, TRC_Score)

Care Coordination Score

CareCoord_Score = TRC_Score

Behavioral Score

Behavioral_Score = GSD_Score

Utilization Score

Built from domain-specific scores like AdmitScore, ERScore, ReadmissionScore, CostScore:

Utilization_Score = Average(AdmitScore, ERScore, ReadmissionScore, CostScore)

VBC Composite Score (example weighting)

Example domain weights (can be tuned to the contract):

Chronic 40%
Preventive 25%
Care Coord 10%
Behavioral 5%
Utilization & Cost 20%
VBC_Score =
  0.40 * Chronic_Score +
  0.25 * Preventive_Score +
  0.10 * CareCoord_Score +
  0.05 * Behavioral_Score +
  0.20 * Utilization_Score

Final_VBC_Score (0–100) = VBC_Score * 100

🏅 Provider Tiering (Example)

Providers can be classified into tiers based on Final VBC Score:

  • Gold: Final VBC Score ≥ 80
  • Silver: 65–79
  • Bronze: < 65

Tiers support incentives, recognition, and targeted improvement programs.

🔥 Key Takeaways

  • Chronic Score has the highest impact on VBC.
  • PMPM is the main cost metric.
  • ER / 1000 & Admit / 1000 show avoidable use.
  • Gap Closure % drives quality performance.

🧠 How to Read Scores

  • Domain scores (Chronic, Preventive, etc.) are 0–1.
  • Final VBC Score is 0–100.
  • Higher = better quality and value.
  • Use tiering (Gold/Silver/Bronze) for quick comparison.

💡 Interpretation Hints

  • Low Chronic + high ER/Admit → poor chronic management.
  • High Preventive but low Chronic → early work done, control still weak.
  • Low TRC & high readmission → poor care coordination.
  • High GSD gaps → consider mental health outreach.
Note: This glossary is for analytics and VBC interpretation only. Clinical specifications follow HEDIS/plan policy.
