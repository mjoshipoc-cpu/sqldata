Member & Provider dimensions Member_ID – Unique identifier for each member/patient. Age – Member age (in years) at the time of measurement. Gender – Member gender (e.g., M/F/Other). Provider_ID – Unique identifier for each servicing provider. Provider_Name – Name of the provider (doctor/clinic). Tin_name – Name associated with the Tax Identification Number (TIN) group. Provider_TIN – Tax Identification Number for the billing provider/group. Provider_Address – Street address of the provider. Provider_city – City where provider is located. Provider_state – State where provider is located. Provider_zip – ZIP code of provider location. Provider_county – County where provider is located. Provider_Phone – Contact phone number of the provider. Provider_Specialty – Specialty (e.g., PCP, Cardiology, Endocrinology). Clinical measurements BP_Systolic – Systolic blood pressure reading. BP_Diastolic – Diastolic blood pressure reading. HbA1c – Latest HbA1c value for diabetic control (%). LDL – Latest LDL cholesterol value (mg/dL). Utilization & cost ER_Visits – Count of emergency room visits for the period. Hospital_Admits – Number of hospital admissions for the period. Readmission_30_Day – Indicator/count of readmissions within 30 days. PMPM_Cost – Per Member Per Month total cost (medical spend). HEDIS-like gap & score columns (Gap typically: 1 = open gap / non-compliant, 0 = closed; Score = performance value) BPD_Gap – Gap flag for Blood Pressure control measure. BPD_Score – Performance score for BPD (e.g., 0–1 or 0–100). CBP_Gap – Gap flag for Controlling High Blood Pressure measure. CBP_Score – Performance score for CBP. COA_Gap – Gap flag for Care for Older Adults measure. COA_Score – Performance score for COA. GSD_Gap – Gap flag for some behavioral/other measure (GSD). GSD_Score – Performance score for GSD. LSC_Gap – Gap flag for lung/smoking cessation or similar measure. LSC_Score – Performance score for LSC. PPC_Gap – Gap flag for Prenatal/Postpartum Care measure. PPC_Score – Performance score for PPC. TRC_Gap – Gap flag for Transition of Care measure. TRC_Score – Performance score for TRC. WCC_Gap – Gap flag for Weight Assessment & Counseling / Well-child visits. WCC_Score – Performance score for WCC. Time dimensions & additional gaps Year – Measurement year (e.g., 2024, 2025). Month – Measurement month (1–12 or month name). A1c_gap – Gap flag specifically for HbA1c control (diabetes measure). LDL_gap – Gap flag for LDL control (cholesterol measure.
