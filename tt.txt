Claims and clinical data tell us what happened. SDOH.ai reveals why it happened, predicts what may happen next, and recommends the best intervention to improve member outcomes while reducing avoidable healthcare costs.

This will be much more impactful than a capability list because it directly answers: Why do we need SDOH if we already have Claims & Clinical data? → Claims tell us what happened; SDOH tells us why and what to do next.
