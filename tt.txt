. Opening (60 sec)
"

Most health plans score members on claims and clinical history alone. That misses people who look low-risk on paper but are high-risk because of where they live. They may have food insecurity, unstable housing, no job, or no transportation. Claims-only models find them when they show up in the ER, which is too late.
This platform adds social determinants of health (SDOH) to the clinical picture and produces one risk score. It identifies who needs help and what kind. It then drives outreach through to a confirmed, closed intervention.

For this solution currently we are using two free Snowflake Marketplace datasets. The clinical and claims data is Synthea, which is synthetic. The neighborhood data is real U.S. Census data. No real PHI is involved."


We train two XGBoost models. The BASE model uses about 51 clinical and financial features. The FULL model uses those plus 39 SDOH features. Each predicts Critical, High, Moderate, or Low.
SDOH Lift is the FULL score minus the BASE score for the same member. It shows how much social data changed their risk. We excluded the composite disadvantage score from training because it risks leakage. SHAP gives the top five drivers for each member, so we can explain each score individually."

4. Outreach lifecycle (3 min)
[Open Outreach] "Here is the full lifecycle of that intervention, and the messages adapt to what she says.
1.	Screening call, 48 hours. She replies 'yes please schedule' and is tagged Engaged.
2.	Refer to program, 72 hours. The message says 'you'd mentioned you'd like to schedule', so it was regenerated from her reply. She answers 'thanks, for refer', which is ambiguous, so it's tagged Unclear. The system doesn't guess.
3.	2-week check-in. The AI decision reads: 'Proceed. Intent unclear, proceeding with the standard next step.' When it's unsure, it takes the safe default and says so.
4.	30-day outcome check. She says 'yes I have completed, thanks.' That is unambiguous, so the gap is tagged Closed.
The amber 'Possible delivery signal' tags come from a keyword check, not an AI judgment. The system never auto-closes on a text match. The care manager types a real confirmation, such as 'Navigator confirmed enrollment by phone 7/15', and clicks Mark Delivered. The server rejects the request without that note. The AI surfaces the signal and a person confirms the fact.
Finally, the care manager chooses Closed, Escalate, or Retry. Retry re-decides the channel and campaign from scratch rather than resending the same plan."

8. Guardrails (use if asked about hallucination)
1.	Ground-truth flags. We pre-compute facts like High Cost Member = Yes/No and give them to the LLM as labeled truth. Telling it not to call someone high-cost failed, so we replaced judgment with a fact.
2.	Explicit prohibitions. The prompts say 'do not speculate about income, cost, or utilization.'
3.	Data minimization. The message composer never sees risk score, tier, or cost.
4.	Deterministic where possible. The campaign planner, care manager summary, and Z-code mapping use no LLM at all.
5.	No generation at send time. Messages are generated when the reply is submitted. What the care manager reviews is what goes out.
6.	Explicit uncertainty. Low confidence produces 'Unclear' and a safe default.
7.	Human confirmation gates. The server rejects delivery confirmation without a typed note.
8.	Single source of truth. get_member_needs() feeds both the UI and the agents, so they can't disagree.
9.	Auditability. Every derived code carries its source field.
10.	Graceful degradation. If the LLM fails, the last good message stays.
"Where a decision could be deterministic, it is. Where an LLM was needed, it's constrained with facts and prohibitions."

Q&A cheat sheet (short answers)
Question	Answer
Real patient data?	No. Clinical data is Synthea (synthetic). Only the Census layer is real.
Snowflake Cortex or Groq?	Verify in the repo first. The code is configured for Cortex COMPLETE(), but your notes say Groq.
Bias or fairness audit?	Not done for this POC. A production rollout needs disparate-impact testing, since SDOH correlates with race and geography.
Retraining cadence?	Not established. The SP_TRAIN_SDOH procedure exists but has no schedule. This POC shows the architecture.
LLM down or slow?	The static message stays. The workflow doesn't block.
Cost at 100k+ members?	Not modeled yet. It needs LLM cost per member times re-run frequency.
FDA clinical decision support?	It scores social and financial risk, not diagnosis, so it's likely out of scope. That is a legal and compliance call, so I wouldn't assert it.
Can a human override the AI?	Yes. Draft/Approve/Reject/Edit, message editing, manual delivery confirmation, and Escalate/Retry.
Do agents disagree?	No. get_member_needs() is computed once and read by both the UI and the agents.
Why show state lift near zero?	It shows the model isn't directionally biased at scale. The signal is at the member and ZIP level.
Are the ROI dollars real?	Programme Cost is benchmark assumptions. Live Referral Impact is real session data.
Auto-close from an SMS reply?	No. The keyword flag only suggests, and the server requires a human-typed note.
Stale message after a reply?	Messages regenerate at reply time, never at send time.
